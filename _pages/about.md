# Research Proposal

**State-Evolution Consistency in Video World Models**

My proposed research studies whether a generated world continues to evolve coherently while objects are outside the camera's view. For example, a cup being filled should show a higher water level when the camera returns.

I am exploring how to decouple state evolution from camera motion, supported by an evaluation pipeline for consistency across changes in viewpoint. The goal is to develop physically plausible, temporally coherent world models and long-video generation systems, and to support creative tools with stable character and scene continuity, plausible motion, and precise control.
