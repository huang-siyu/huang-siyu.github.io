I am a fourth-year undergraduate student in **Intelligence Science and Technology** at **Sun Yat-sen University**. My research interests include **world models**, **long-video generation**, and **reliable generative models**. I am currently working with [Dr. Anyi Rao](https://anyirao.com/) on state-evolution consistency in video world models.

My research has progressed from robust vision-language representations and image generation to interactive world modelling. I am interested in building systems that maintain coherent states and physically plausible dynamics over long sequences, and in applying them to filmmaking and interactive virtual environments.
